DESKTOP NEKO — QUIET COMPANY

Intent
  A curious little presence at the edge of your work. Pleasant to notice,
  perfectly happy to be ignored. Keep the original pixel cat and a tiny footprint.

Interaction rules
  Visual reactions before words. No attention debt, chores, streaks, or guilt.
  Drawn sequences and connected reactions; optional short cursor walks and soft sounds.
  Petting is deliberate: a click, not a drag or an accidental hover.
  Window curiosity has an eight-second cooldown; cursor curiosity uses 18 seconds.
  Typing bursts earn paw taps and local questions, throttled to 30 seconds.
  Never read key values or typed text. Local title hashes help notice app tab changes.
  Idle gestures default to about 14 seconds; configurable from five to 120 seconds.
  Sleep delay defaults to three minutes; configurable from one to 3600 seconds.
  Timer edits commit on Enter or focus loss; controls, loading and saving share bounds.
  Eight frames lower her into sleep; a pet or optional nap deadline plays the eight-frame wake.
  Typing, window changes and dragging do not wake her; the optional nap deadline does.
  Exactly twelve four-frame pet responses rotate through a shuffled bag before repeating.
  Keep head pats, cheek nuzzle, kneading, chin scritches, blep and ear rubs.
  Two 4-column by 3-row sheets add front stretch, back arch, hop, side flop,
  shoulder peek and a corrected paw offer. No six-frame petting reactions.
  Nap duration is independently configurable: 1–3600 seconds or 0 until petted (default).
  Nine idle moods avoid consecutive repeats.
  298 offline lines use context shuffle bags, with personal questions and app rituals.
  Local foreground app names and media title hints select lines and matching gestures.
  Shared speech cooldown prevents spam. No recording or actual playback detection.
  Ambient thoughts default to 75 seconds; configurable from 15 to 600. Quiet removes them.
  Cursor walks move only left/right at the same height, keep a gap, last at most 6.5 seconds and travel about 220 pixels maximum.
  Eligible moving cursors invite another walk after 25 seconds.
  Keep 5–6 second gesture slots: one natural gesture followed by a relaxed rest.
  No repeated grooming, stretching, waving or head-bobbing within one gesture slot.
  Tail swishes, kneading and paw taps use gentle continuous cycles.
  Rendering runs at 10 FPS, preserving action lengths and cursor-walk speed.
  Standing drawings keep a consistent size; transitions use one fixed character scale.
  Original soft mews, purrs and chirps are opt-in. Include a Listen button and stop toggle.
  Automatic sound has a 45-second pause; quiet/fullscreen/media suppress it.
  Hiding is absolute. Fullscreen hiding is optional; native pinning never steals focus.
  Fullscreen activity always suppresses unsolicited speech and activity reactions.
  Motion can be disabled without losing interaction.

Small home
  One nook for preferences, startup, AI, lifetime pets and naps.
  Remember position and clamp it into an available monitor.
  112 loaded drawings including twelve pet reactions and sleep/wake transitions.
  Place the menu and nook beside her. Skip topmost reassertion while these are open.
  Startup is opt-in; a single-instance lock prevents duplicate pets.

Optional AI
  Off by default. Current window title only; no screenshots or history.
  One request at a time, at most once every ten minutes; bounded timeout.
  Discard stale replies after interaction, hiding, focus mode or fullscreen.
  The offline pet remains complete when the service is unavailable.

Deliberately excluded
  Voice/chat UI, hunger systems, notifications, task reminders, cloud memory,
  roaming across windows, new accounts, plugin systems, and new art dependencies.
  Browser extensions and exact background-tab tracking stay out of this pass.

Validation
  Deterministic behavior tests, Qt interaction tests with temporary settings,
  mocked AI requests, native Windows rendering, and a packaged-executable smoke test.
