# Neko 1.2: sound brief

The current three cues (`mew`, `purr`, `chirp`) were synthesized in code (`tools/make_sounds.py`)
and nobody loved them. This brief is for hand-picking real kitten sounds. Sounds stay **off by
default**, as now, and every rule about Quiet company, fullscreen, media apps and the 45-second
automatic pause still applies.

## Where to get them
- **Freesound.org**, filtered to licence **CC0** (no attribution needed). CC-BY is fine too if
  you note the author; I'll keep a credits file.
- **Pixabay sound effects**: free licence, no attribution.
- Your own recordings of a real kitten. These are the best if you have one around.
- Avoid ripped game or anime audio, and "free" sites without a clear licence.

## Format (please match)
- **WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono.** MP3 or OGG are fine too and I'll convert them.
- Trim the silence: start within 20 ms of the sound, with a short fade-out at the end.
- **Quiet and close:** no room echo, no background TV, wind or hum, no other animals or people.
- Rough loudness doesn't matter. I normalize everything to the same soft level.
- **Kitten, not adult cat:** higher, smaller and softer. If a clip sounds like a big tomcat,
  skip it.
- Name each file as listed below and put it in `assets-incoming/sounds/`. Two or three
  variants per cue are great (`mew-1.wav`, `mew-2.wav`…): she picks randomly, so she doesn't
  sound like a loop.

## The cues

| File | Length | What it should sound like | When she uses it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mew` | 0.3–0.7 s | a tiny, soft, rising "mew?", gentle and friendly | a deliberate pet; the nook's Listen button |
| `purr` | 2–4 s, **loopable** | a steady, soft, close purr with no meow in it; the start and end should blend so it can repeat | while being petted |
| `chirp` | 0.2–0.5 s | the bright "brrp!" / trill cats make when greeting | arrival; "!" when she notices the arrow |
| `trill-happy` | 0.4–0.9 s | an excited rolling "mrrrp!", happy and bouncy | game wins, forgiveness after restless |
| `mrrp-question` | 0.3–0.6 s | a short curious "mrr?" with an upward ending | dizzy; "what's that?" moments |
| `yawn` | 0.6–1.2 s | a kitten yawn, a little squeak at the end | falling asleep, waking up |
| `protest` | 0.4–0.9 s | a short, cute complaining "mrrow!" (not angry or hissing) | "put me down", "no fair!", false starts |
| `sad-mew` | 0.5–1.0 s | a small, falling, sad "mew…" | losing a game, sulky curl |
| `demand-meow` | 0.5–1.0 s | a louder, insistent "MEOW!" that's still kitten-sized | restless: Jealous and Demanding stages |
| `giggle-chatter` | 0.4–1.0 s | the fast little chattering/chirping cats make when excited | Dodge: after escaping, when tagged |

**Don't** send hisses, growls, yowls (mating calls) or distressed crying. They read as a hurt
animal, not a playful one.

## What I'll do with them
- Normalize all of them to the same soft level, with a safety limit so nothing is loud.
- Map each cue to its moments.
- Keep the existing 45 s automatic pause and the "deliberate actions may play sooner" rule.
- The **purr** gets a fade in and out while petting, instead of a one-shot.
- Ship the files inside the build, plus a `docs/sound-credits.md` with every source and licence.
- The old synthesized cues are retired, but `tools/make_sounds.py` stays as a fallback.
