## 1. Roam engine (shared foundation, `roam.py`)

She moves around **her own monitor** like a real kitten. Moves: walk (exists), gallop
(exists), pounce-hop (M3 art; interim: the hunt jump at ground level), leap up/down (M1/M2
art; interim: the arrival landing), turn, stop and sit (exist). Her "ground" is the height
she sits at; leaps change it.

**Perches:** a coarse grid of spots on the work area (taskbar excluded), about 160 px apart.
There are no window tops.

**Courtesy rules (always on):**
- She never comes to rest within **140 px of the cursor**. In Dodge the cursor is part of
  play, so its own rules apply.
- She never sits over the text caret. Only the caret's rectangle is read, never text.
- She freezes while any mouse button is held anywhere.
- Typing bursts pause her.
- Fullscreen, hide, menus, carrying and naps stop or pause her.
- Gentle motion off means no travel.
- Positions during roaming are never saved, and she always goes home afterwards.

The planner is time-based (stalls pause it; dropped frames don't skip ahead), the same as
Catch.

## 2. Dodge the Cursor

### How she behaves (the cat logic)
Real kittens play **approach-avoid**. They creep toward what interests them, wait, then bolt
the moment it comes at them, and come back to tease again. The game is built on that loop.

### Start (automatic, on its own)
- Uses the same cooldown timer as Catch, and automatic games alternate between Catch and
  Dodge.
- Dodge needs **no approach**. When it's due, she checks that:
  - you are active (the mouse moved in the last 5 s)
  - the cursor is on her monitor
  - you aren't typing, holding a button, in fullscreen or in a menu
- If so, she **notices the cursor wherever it is**: "!" mark, ears up, a turn toward it.
- Manual: Play → Dodge the Cursor.

### Beats
| # | Beat | Length | What she does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Notice** | 0.6 s | "!" mark, looks at the cursor |
| 2 | **Announce** | 2.0 s | declaration line; seated, then the tease crouch (D1) |
| 3 | **Countdown** | 3 s | "3… 2… 1…", butt-wiggle; then "Go!" |
| 4 | **Approach** | until close | creeps or walks toward the cursor, stopping about 180 px away; play-bow and "come on" paw swipe (D1) |
| 5 | **Play** (30 s) | | see the loop below |
| 6 | **Result** | 3–5 s | win (W1/W2) or good-sport flop (D5) with lines |
| 7 | **Home** | | run, then walk home, then settle (as Catch now) |

### Play loop (every frame)
- **Threat** = the cursor's distance to her body, and its speed toward her.
- **Tell:** the cursor comes within about 110 px, or rushes at her (over about 600 px/s
  toward her). She leans away and her ears go back (D2 drawings 2–3) for **250–350 ms**.
  That's your window to tag her.
- **Commit to one escape**, chosen by room and threat direction. She doesn't read the
  future:
  - **side-step hop** (D2): 40–70 px away from the cursor
  - **duck** (D3): the cursor is coming from above
  - **dash**: a gallop 150–300 px away, when the cursor keeps chasing
  - **leap** (M1/M2): to another height, when cornered vertically
  - **cornered** (an edge behind her): she dashes *past* the cursor with a hop. That's
    risky, and most tags happen here.
- **Recovery:** after each escape she can't dodge for **0.7–1.0 s**. That's fair, and she
  can't chain perfect escapes.
- **Tease:** if the cursor hangs back for over 3 s, she creeps closer again and play-bows
  ("over here!").
- **Tag:** a tag counts only if:
  - the arrow is on her painted body (an alpha test of the real frame, the same test as
    Catch's paw check); the tail tip and ear tips don't count
  - it stays there for **at least 120 ms**
  - she isn't inside her **1.2 s after-tag grace** (she flees during that)
- **Brushes that don't count** get a line and no tag. There is at most one such line every
  4 s.
- **Clock:** 30 s from "Go!". At 10 s and 3 s left she taunts. Three tags = you win; the
  clock runs out = she wins.

### Fouls and fails
| Situation | Rule | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Touching her during the **announcement or countdown** | 1st time: warning, countdown restarts. 2nd time: foul | Neko wins by forfeit (counts as her win) |
| Cursor **far away**: over 600 px from her, or on another monitor | after 1.5 s a warning line; after 4 s continuous | Neko wins by forfeit |
| Cursor stops moving for 8 s during play | she gets bored | round ends, **no score** (you walked off) |
| Pet, carry, hide, fullscreen, End game, menu | neutral, same as Catch (menus pause) | cancelled, no score |
| Typing burst during play | pause, with a line; if it lasts over 5 s, cancel | no score |

### Counting
Dodge has **its own** "games played" and "Neko wins" counters, shown alongside Catch's in

mid-duck. The same 3-tag, 30 s rules apply.

## 4. Restless kitten (on by default)

### Settings (Company tab)
- **"Restless when ignored"**: on by default.
- **"Restless after"**: 5–120 minutes, default **20**.
- Quiet company also stops it starting.

### Trigger
- She's awake and idle.
- You're **active at the computer** (input within the nap delay). If you're away, she naps.
- No **pet, game or carry** for the set time. The clock only counts while you're active.
- Not during fullscreen, typing bursts, menus, games or the nook (Journal tab excepted).

### Stages (agitation rises while ignored, falls with pets)
| Stage | Behaviour | Rhythm | Art |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Restless** | glances at you, sighs (R1), a short walk and back | rest 25–40 s | R1 |
| **Pouncy** | 1–2 pounce-hops or a gallop, then **stops, says something, relaxes** | burst 3–6 s, rest 12–20 s | M3 |
| **Jealous** | bursts **toward the cursor**, stopping at the 140 px gap; glares at it, huffs (R2) | burst 5–9 s, rest 6–10 s | R2 |
| **Demanding** | keeps pouncing near the cursor; "pet me!" paw-taps and head-butts (R3) | rest 2–4 s | R3 |
| **Soft cap** | after 10 min of Demanding with no response: curls up and sulks with the rain cloud **until petted** | — | existing curl |

**Getting your attention:** in the Jealous and Demanding stages, each burst heads for the
spot nearest your cursor that keeps the gap. She stays on your side of the screen, at about
the cursor's height (leaping if needed), and turns to face you.

### Calming her
- Pets needed: Restless 1 · Pouncy 2 · Jealous 3 · Demanding 4 · Soft-cap sulk 5.
- She reacts after each pet with a "calm_progress" line, then when satisfied plays the
  forgive melt (R4) with hearts, and **goes home**.
- **Carrying** pauses her but doesn't calm her: "hey! that's not a pet."
- A finished **game** round calms her by one stage.
- After calming, the ignore clock starts again from zero.

## 6. Neko 1.2 release

- Builds to **`dist/DesktopNeko-1.2.exe`**: one flat file next to the stable
  `DesktopNeko.exe` and `DesktopNeko-previous.exe`, so the current build and its predecessor
  stay untouched. Each 1.2 rebuild replaces only that file. (AGENTS.md is updated to allow
  this third file.)
- **Same settings and the same single-instance lock** as the stable build. Your position,
  pets and counters carry over. New 1.2 settings are simply ignored by the older exe.
  Only one Neko runs at a time. Quit one before starting the other.
- Code: commit the current stable state first, then do 1.2 on its own branch
  (`neko-1.2`), so the stable build can still be rebuilt unchanged.
- Plan.txt rule updates:
  - "happy to be ignored" becomes *"quiet by default; an opt-in-off restless mood asks for
    attention; never while you're away, in fullscreen or in Quiet company"*
  - "no roaming across windows" becomes *"no perching on windows; movement stays on her
    monitor's work area"*
