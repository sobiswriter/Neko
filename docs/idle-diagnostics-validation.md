## Findings and corrections

The base idle holds and blinks; the other gestures already contain anticipation/action/recovery. Canonical endpoint substitutions keep identity stable but do not prove connected weight changes or turns. The proposed refresh prioritizes supported resting breath/blinks, loaf and stretch, then grooming/yawn/peek, followed by the smaller ear/sniff/look/lazy-swish actions. The permanent video/spine/silhouette references inform mechanics, not Neko's design. New artwork requires the user's review of that plan. Tail chase/catch art, cadence, entry/recovery, weights and rest are unchanged.

Gesture thoughts previously competed with general/activity speech through the same chatter pause. Now a matching active gesture can speak independently and does not reset/consume the general timer. Its bubble lifetime is capped to the remaining gesture. Stale gesture keys are rejected. General thoughts use the configured interval, with no0.8–1.3 jitter, and remain due while another bubble blocks display. They can be dispatched independently of the behavior model choosing a gesture that tick, preventing short idle-gesture settings from starving general thoughts. Actual display acknowledges the next general interval; optional AI request/response also records its own progress. Editing an unrelated timing setting no longer restarts both speech and gesture deadlines.

The single readable bubble, quiet, fullscreen, sleep/care and prompted-interaction rules remain. A gesture starting while another thought is visible can stay silent; it is logged as deferred and never queued to speak out of context later. A due general thought waits instead of being silently spent. Activity responses keep their own existing shared pause.

The automatic timer previously cleared outside-zone history until an intent was pending. An otherwise valid approach at the cooldown boundary could therefore have no qualifying outside history, or appear as a short outside flicker. It now tracks eligible outside/entry history before expiry while still requiring the entry itself at/after the due time and a250ms stable hover. A cursor already inside at expiry must leave/re-enter. Sleep/care, typing/buttons, hidden/fullscreen/quiet and other-monitor gates still clear stale qualification and coalesce one pending intent. Gesture recovery can finish before play begins.

Opening the Nook also blocked auto play, making live verification awkward. Other Nook controls still pause automatic invitations; **Journal** can observe live play. An explicit manual Play from the Nook is allowed. Wait reasons distinguish controls/menu open, care/sleep, typing, other monitor, fresh entry, stable hover, cooldown and recovery; model invitation acceptance is separate from actual game creation or a not-enough-room/artwork failure.

## Journal

Neko's nook → Journal provides a read-only, category-filtered live log and Copy log. Entries include timezone-aware millisecond timestamps, actual displayed thought text/channel, idle/sleep/care/lifecycle state and clip changes, activity reaction types, game configuration/invitation/phase/result and stop/reset decisions. Repeated polling wait reasons are deduplicated; actual repeated actions remain events. No raw keys, typed text, raw window titles or cursor trails are recorded.

Real app logs live in its local app-data folder as `neko-events.jsonl`; the Journal tooltip exposes the full path. INI-backed tests/probes use their temporary settings directory. Memory retains up to500 entries. At512KB the current file rotates to `neko-events.previous.jsonl`, replacing that one older file. Valid recent rows reload across sessions; malformed rows are ignored. Disk failures keep the current visible in-memory log and show a clear UI error, recovering on a later successful write. There is no upload or additional runtime dependency.

## Limits

The automatic test accelerates the due deadline around a real timed entry; it does not spend90 seconds per run. Existing tests cover the actual90-second model deadline. Physical mixed-DPI/multi-monitor trials, long soak, logon/reboot and real Gemini requests were not added. No proposed animation has been generated or approved. Logging records actions/decisions, not every repaint, and bounded rotation eventually replaces older history. A visible bubble or care/quiet state can intentionally delay speech or invitations; the log explains those conditions.
